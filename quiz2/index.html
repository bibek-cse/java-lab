2. How it works

  • You see one question at a time. The questions and options are shuffled every time you start.
  • Tap an answer. Green means correct and red means wrong, and a simple explanation appears.
  • Most questions ask for the output. Code without a class runs inside main(). "Compilation error" and "Runtime error" can be correct answers.
  • Keyboard: press 1–4 to answer, → for next, ← for back. On a phone you can swipe.
  • Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Your total marks appear at the end.
